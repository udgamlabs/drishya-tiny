Drishya Tiny in your browser

Drishya Tiny is a 212M-parameter video generator trained from scratch, using the frozen Wan2.2 VAE and Flan-T5-Base text encoder. Here it runs entirely inside this tab: type a prompt, optionally add a photo as the first frame, and it makes a 2-second 256×256 clip. The model files download once and are kept by your browser.

Needs a laptop or desktop with a recent Chrome or Edge (WebGPU). Checking this device…
The photo is cropped to a centre square and stays on this device; it is never uploaded.

Runs on your device; nothing you type leaves your browser, and photos never leave your device.

The browser version uses a tiny decoder (TAEW2.2), so clips are softer than the full model's. It works best with long, descriptive prompts of nature and scenery. Where it falls short: it follows prompts about half as well as larger peers, rarely draws two named objects, faces and hands smear, text melts, and motion is small. Every clip carries a visible "AI-generated" mark.
