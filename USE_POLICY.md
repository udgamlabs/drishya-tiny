# Drishya Tiny: use policy

Drishya Tiny is a small research model. It makes short, low-resolution clips that are often wrong. We release it so
people can study and learn from a fully open, low-cost video model. Please use it in that spirit.

## Please don't use Drishya Tiny, or anything built from it, to

1. Break the law, or help anyone else break it.
2. Make sexual content involving minors, or any content that sexualises minors.
3. Make sexual or intimate content of a real person without their consent.
4. Impersonate a real person, or make realistic video of a real person saying or doing things they did not say or do,
   without their consent.
5. Deceive people: for example, passing generated video off as real footage, or as news, evidence or a record of real
   events.
6. Harass, threaten, defame or bully anyone, or target people based on protected characteristics.
7. Create disinformation meant to influence elections or public safety.
8. Make decisions about people's rights, jobs, credit, health or legal status.

## Please do

- **Label outputs as AI-generated** wherever you show them, and keep any provenance metadata our tools add.
- **Credit** "Drishya Tiny by Udgam Labs" and keep the licence notice.
- **Report problems** (harmful outputs, misuse, mistakes in our documents) to the contact on the model page.

## Our own demo

Every clip from our hosted demo carries a visible "AI-generated" mark and provenance metadata. The demo is for trying
the model, not for producing content to publish.
